# CrowdStrike agrees to buy Zscaler for $43.9 billion

Before a laptop connects to a company app, a security team needs to know two things: **is the device safe to let in, and what should it be allowed to reach?** CrowdStrike and Zscaler already work together to answer those questions. On November 23, [CrowdStrike announced an agreement to acquire Zscaler](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/crowdstrike-to-acquire-zscaler-bringing-network-security-and-zero-trust-to-the-falcon-platform/) in an **all-stock deal valued at about $43.9 billion**.

## How CrowdStrike and Zscaler work together

**CrowdStrike Falcon** monitors devices such as laptops and servers for threats and can assess their security status. **Zscaler** applies access rules to the connections those devices make. With the [existing device-check integration](https://help.zscaler.com/zscaler-technology-partners/zscaler-and-crowdstrike-deployment-guide), Zscaler can use Falcon’s device assessment when deciding whether to let a laptop reach a private app. The companies say they already have **more than 2,000 shared customers and 16 integrations**.

## What the deal could change

| Deal term | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Enterprise value | About $43.9 billion, based on CrowdStrike’s November 20 closing share price |
| Payment | 1.135 CrowdStrike shares per Zscaler share |
| Target close | Second half of CrowdStrike’s fiscal 2028, subject to regulatory and Zscaler shareholder approvals and other closing conditions |

The two companies say they will **operate independently until closing**. Afterward, CrowdStrike plans to let customers of either company add the other’s products under one **Falcon Flex** contract. It also plans to bring device and network activity into a shared data layer, so an investigation could follow what happened on a laptop and what it tried to reach.

Teams can already use Falcon’s device assessment to inform Zscaler’s access decision. The deal’s value to those teams will come down to whether one contract and a shared view of the evidence make the two tools easier to use together.
