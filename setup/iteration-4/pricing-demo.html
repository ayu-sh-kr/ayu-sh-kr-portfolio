Before you ask

The questions I usually get.

Are these prices final?

No — they're honest starting points. Scope, timeline, and how much I own vs. advise all move the number. The estimator gets you close; a 20-minute call gets you exact.

Can I hire you for both a talk and a build?

Often the best fit. A workshop that turns into an engagement, or a build where I also train your team, usually gets a combined rate. Just tell me the whole picture.

What if my budget is smaller?

Say so. Early-stage startups, non-profits, and community events get real flexibility — equity, reduced rates, or a tighter scope that still ships something useful. I'd rather find a way than lose the conversation.

Do I keep the code and the infrastructure?

Always. It's your code, your AWS account, your docs. I build things you can walk away from — a backend you can't hand off isn't finished.
