Questions

The long answers.

Everything clients and library users actually ask, written out once so nobody has to wait on me for it.

What counts as support, and what counts as new work? +
Getting help If something I built stopped doing what we agreed it would do, that's support and it's on me. If it works as specified but you now want it to do something else, that's new work — a change request, a retainer task, or a small build. I'll tell you which bucket yours is in the first reply, and I won't quietly bill you for the first kind.
How fast do you actually reply? +
Getting help Median first reply is about four hours during the week. Worst case is one working day. If something is live-affecting, email with URGENT in the subject instead of using the form — that one routes to my phone and gets picked up outside desk hours.
Is support included after a project ends? +
Getting help Yes — 30 days of bug-fix support ships with every build, starting the day it goes live. Defects found in that window are fixed at no cost. After 30 days, a retainer covers you, or you can send one-off fixes my way and I'll quote them hourly.
Can we get on a call instead of typing? +
Getting help Often, yes, and sometimes it's faster. Send the message first with a line saying a call would help — I'll reply with a booking link for a 20-minute slot. Starting in writing means I can look at the thing before we talk, so the call is spent fixing rather than describing.
What do you need from me to move fast? +
Getting help Four things beat a perfect bug report: what you expected, what happened instead, when it started, and the exact error text pasted rather than described. A 15-second screen recording is worth more than three paragraphs. Environment matters too — staging and production fail differently.
Someone else on my team hit this. Can they write in? +
Getting help Anyone on your side can use this page — there's no account and no allowlist. For anything that touches billing, access, or credentials, I'll loop in whoever signed the engagement before acting.
Do you offer retainers, and what's in one? +
Scope & retainers Yes. A retainer buys a fixed block of hours a month covering fixes, small changes, dependency and security updates, monitoring review, and a standing slot to talk through what's next. Unused hours don't roll over indefinitely — one month of carry-over, then they lapse, because hoarding them turns into a crunch nobody enjoys.
I need a change, not a fix. What happens? +
Scope & retainers Describe the shape of it and I'll come back with an honest estimate and the tradeoffs I see — including the version of it that's half the work and gets you 80% there. Nothing starts until you've agreed to a number. If it fits inside your retainer hours, I'll say so.
Will you support code you didn't write? +
Scope & retainers Sometimes. It starts with a short paid audit so I can see what I'd be signing up for — usually a day or two. After that I'll tell you plainly whether I can maintain it, what I'd want to change first, and what I'd rather not touch. A no comes with reasons and, where I can, a referral.
What's explicitly not covered? +
Scope & retainers Third-party outages I can't control, content and copy changes made by your team, training beyond the handover session, and anything that requires credentials you haven't given me. I'll still help you find the right thread to pull — I just can't promise a fix for it.
Where do I report a bug in a dota library? +
dota libraries Open a GitHub issue on the relevant repo — public issues help the next person hit the same wall and find the answer already there. Include the library version, browser, and a minimal reproduction. Use the form on this page instead only if the report would expose something private.
Do you take pull requests? +
dota libraries Gladly. Open an issue first for anything larger than a typo so we can agree on the approach before you spend an evening on it. Keep the diff focused, match the existing style, and add a test if the change touches behaviour.
How do versions and breaking changes work? +
dota libraries Semver, honestly applied. Breaking changes only land in majors, and every major ships with a migration note and a codemod where one is possible. The previous major keeps getting security patches for six months after a new one lands.
Which browsers do the libraries support? +
dota libraries The last two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari, plus iOS Safari and Chrome on Android. Everything degrades to working-but-plainer rather than broken when a newer CSS or platform feature isn't available, and reduced-motion preferences are respected throughout.
Can I use them commercially? +
dota libraries Yes — MIT, no attribution required, no strings. They're free software with no support guarantee attached; issues get answered on a best-effort basis, usually within a week. If you need a response time you can plan around, that's a retainer conversation.
How does invoicing work? +
Billing & handover Invoices come from me directly, not a portal you have to log into. Project work is billed against milestones; retainers go out on the first of the month. Terms are 14 days, and bank transfer or card both work. Need a past invoice re-sent? Message me with the rough date and it'll be in your inbox the same day.
We lost access to the AWS account. Now what? +
Billing & handover The account is yours and always was — it's registered to your organisation, not to me. Root recovery goes through AWS with the billing contact on file. If I still hold an IAM role there, I can help your admin re-establish access, and we'll rotate everything afterwards.
Can you transfer the repository to our org? +
Billing & handover That's the default at handover, not a favour. You get the repo, the infrastructure definitions, the docs, and a walkthrough for whoever's picking it up. Nothing is held hostage and there's no lock-in — the point is that you could hire someone else tomorrow without a rescue mission.
I found a security issue. Where do I report it? +
Security & data Email security@ayush.dev rather than opening a public issue or using this form. You'll get an acknowledgement within 72 hours and honest updates while it's being fixed. I'll credit you when it's disclosed unless you'd rather I didn't.
What happens to what I send through this form? +
Security & data It becomes an email to me and nothing else — no ticketing SaaS, no analytics on the contents, no third party in the middle. Attachments are deleted once the thread is closed. Please don't paste live credentials or customer records; a redacted log tells me just as much. The privacy policy has the full detail.
Can you sign our NDA, DPA, or vendor forms? +
Security & data Usually yes, and quickly — most standard NDAs come back signed the same day. Send it over with the first message. For longer vendor security questionnaires, I'll fill them in but I'll be candid about which controls a one-person shop genuinely has and which ones I'd be pretending about.
